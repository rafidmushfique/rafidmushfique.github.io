About

I study how computing systems can sense and respond to people's emotional and cognitive states, such as stress, cognitive load and attention, using physiological signals from wearable and mobile devices.

I am completing a Master of Information Technology (Professional Computing) at Swinburne University of Technology, finishing at the end of 2026. My undergraduate thesis at BRAC University used ECG and GSR signals from the AMIGOS dataset to detect stress automatically with machine learning.

Before graduate study I spent several years as a software engineer, building enterprise systems used every day in manufacturing, HR and banking. That work taught me to build complete, dependable systems around real users, and I want to bring that to research prototypes and user studies in HCI.

Looking for a PhD position. I am applying for fully funded PhD positions in human-computer interaction, affective computing and ubiquitous sensing, starting after I finish my master's in early 2027. Get in touch.
